Equipment view and launcher (MoP Classic, Interface 50504)

Core registers the WowSimGearSync LibDataBroker launcher. Left-click toggles the
equipment view; right-click opens Blizzard options. LibDBIcon uses
db.profile.minimap with hide=true and minimapPos=225 defaults. AceDB callbacks
refresh its profile binding. SavedVariables persist position and settings.
Bundled broker licenses and provenance remain under Libs.

The 350 by 550 equipment view renders Equipment State targets and comparisons.
It has 16 reusable item buttons, an editable active-import name, Import, and the
global Equip/Sync Set control. The name editor commits on Enter and cancels on
Escape or focus loss. Successful imports close and clear the editor; failures
retain the pasted JSON and show a readable error.
Names and textures use client item data. Unresolved names use loading text.

A confirmed item mismatch, including an empty equipped slot, has a red border
and the imported target icon. Modification icons and completion checks stay
hidden until identity matches. UNKNOWN remains distinct from missing gear.
Hover explains availability/blockers; clicking routes to selected-slot planning.
Rings/trinkets use resolved physical assignments. Hiding icons does not change
underlying comparison fields or summary counts.

Matching items show applicable modification icons with current/target tooltips.
Item clicks route to the next available modification. Completed items show a
completion check and normal Blizzard inventory tooltip. Reforge and upgrade
icons direct the player to the corresponding NPC workflow.

Equipment owns observation invalidation and item-data requests. UI messages
coalesce relevant visible-window refreshes with C_Timer.After(0). Equipment,
bag, lock, cursor, combat, skill, and spell changes refresh relevant presentation.
Item-data completions are requester-filtered. Hidden equipment views do not
poll; execution owners can still verify pending operations. The import editor's
scrolling EditBox update handler is separate from inventory observation.
