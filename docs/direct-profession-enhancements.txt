Direct profession enhancements (MoP Classic, Interface 50504)

Execution contract

Direct item-targeting recipes reuse the slot-click sequence used for
Engineering: open the profession through its profession spellbook button, find
the exact recipe link, verify reagents/tools/cooldown and selection, call
DoTradeSkill, and target the equipped item with UseInventoryItem. No protected
step is deferred to an event or timer. Events correlate casts, verify the equipped result, and close the TradeSkill
frame after a matching UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED.

Inscription boundary

Secret Inscription profession recipes create BoP enhancement items instead of
starting item targeting. WSGS does not automate that intermediate crafting. Already-carried finished items remain usable:
4912/87581 (Ox Horn), 4913/87585 (Tiger Fang), 4914/87584 (Tiger Claw), and
4915/87582 (Crane Wing).

Socket observation

EquipmentScanner observes the equipped link with C_Item.GetItemNumSockets and
C_Item.GetItemNumAddedSockets, and considers populated link gem positions.
The comparator compares the highest imported positive gem position against the
observed socket count. Wrist/hands socket work applies only to Blacksmiths;
without that profession the prerequisite is NOT_APPLICABLE. An unavailable count
stays UNKNOWN. A verified socket prerequisite allows gem planning to advance.
